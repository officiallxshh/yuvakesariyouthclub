YUVAKESARI YOUTH CLUB

Privacy Policy

Effective from 25 September 2026

This page explains, in simple terms, how information submitted through the Yuvakesari Youth Club (YYC) website is used for membership, administration and club activities.

Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, the website may collect details such as your name, date of birth, phone number, email address, photograph, membership or role information, and other information you choose to submit.

How information is used

Information is used to process membership applications, maintain member and leader records, provide account access, manage approvals, create digital membership cards, verify membership through the website, and operate YYC activities and communications.

Photos and profile information

Photos and profile details submitted for membership may be used within the website's membership and digital ID features. Please submit only information and photographs that you are authorized to provide.

Storage and service providers

YYC uses third-party infrastructure, including Supabase, to store and process website data. Access is limited through the website's authentication and permission systems, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data sharing

YYC does not use the website as a marketplace for selling member information. Information may be shared only when needed to operate the service, maintain the website, comply with a lawful request, or protect the security of YYC and its users.

Data retention

Membership and administrative records may be kept for as long as they are needed for club operations, record keeping, verification, or legitimate administrative purposes. Retention may vary by record type.

Your choices

Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information. Members can request correction of their submitted profile information through the available account features or by contacting YYC through its official communication channels.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Yuvakesari Youth Club through its official Instagram: instagram.com/yuvakesari__kukke.
